## What changed and why

With a song item selected in 준비, the right column now shows 미리보기 and 항목
설정, as on the `PrepService` board.

- **One selected place.** The chips, the thumbnails, and the preview share the
  selected place in the order the item plays: `ServiceItemStore.position` and
  `selectPosition()`, reset to the first place for another item. Readers clamp
  it with `shownPosition()`.
- **미리보기.** The editor's preview body moved into `SongPreviewFrame`, which
  takes a `SongPicture` (song, 슬라이드, background, place, and count); the
  editor's `SongPreviewPanel` now passes its selected block, and the new
  `ItemPreviewPanel` passes `itemPicture()`: the selected place of the effective
  구성 (an empty 구성 plays file order), on the item's background override or
  else the song's. The caption reads as spec 05 asks: `프리코러스 5 / 9 기본`.
  It draws locally, as the editor's preview does, and sends nothing to the
  output.
- **항목 설정, 원본.** `ItemSettingsPanel` shows the 곡 row: the song title with
  the `link` icon, `라이브러리`, and 라이브러리 곡 열기 (`IconButton` sm,
  `open`), which opens the song in the editor. `PropertyValue` takes that button
  as `action` at the right end of the control cell, as the PropertyRow preview
  card draws it (README and gallery updated). The 이번 예배 group and 메모 come
  in the next request.
- **Mount.** The 준비 right column is 미리보기 and 항목 설정 while no 라이브러리
  song is open.
