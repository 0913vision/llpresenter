## What changed and why

Quick add (항목 추가) now has the 다른 항목 group under the 라이브러리 songs.
Its entries insert the item with the same rule as songs (at the end of the group
of the selected item, or at the end of the list):

- **머리글** inserts a header named `머리글` and opens its name for renaming.
- **빈 자리** opens a submenu with 찬양, 성경 구절, and 설교; each inserts a
  slot of that kind (`song`, `bible`, `slides`) titled with its name.
- **미디어** opens a submenu with the media list (pictures and videos, no
  audio); a choice inserts a media item.
- **공지** opens a submenu with the 공지 sets; a choice inserts a 공지 item. An
  empty submenu shows a disabled `미디어 없음` or `공지 없음`.

성경 구절 as an item of its own waits for the Bible lookup bridge and is a
separate request, as agreed with the lead.

The media list and the 공지 sets are read once each time the popover opens.

**Renaming a header** (spec 05 asks for "rename mode"; the list had none):

- `ListGroup` takes `rename`: the group name becomes a field in place with the
  old name selected. Enter or leaving it saves, Esc cancels, a blank name keeps
  the old one (ListRow README).
- In the list, a double click on a header, 이름 바꾸기 in its right-click menu,
  or a header just inserted by quick add (`state/headerRename`) opens the field.
  A read-only service doesn't rename.
- `ServiceOrderEdits.renameHeader()` saves it as one undo step, keeping the
  header's other fields; `insertItem()` inserts a ready-made item the same way
  as `insertSong`.
