## What changed and why

The narrow 진행 도구 panel shows 표시 중 and 무대 as tabs and puts the rest
behind 더 보기 (Layout README, spec 04). spec-43 drew that button in
`ShowToolsPanel` and copied the selected-tab underline into its own CSS. The
panel parts now own it: `PanelTabs` takes `more={{label, tabs}}` and draws a
ghost sm menu button at the right end of the header. It opens a `Menu` of the
remaining tabs with a check mark before the current one (disabled tabs stay
disabled, with their reason). While one of them is selected, no tab in the row
is, and the button shows as the selected tab with the same 2 px underline. With
no leftover tabs there is no button.

The look follows the `Compact` board and spec-43's reviewed styles: `label`
`text-2`, a 12 px `text-3` arrow, hover `bg-raised`, open `bg-control-hover`.
`ShowToolsPanel` is unchanged; the lead asks the 진행 member to switch to `more`
after both requests merge.
