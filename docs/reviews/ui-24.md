## What changed and why

The song editor now has a pinned 34px header: title field, live slide count,
library tag, and service-use count with service names in its tooltip. The
initial library list cannot overwrite a newer push. Blocks scroll below it.

Blank and exact duplicate titles show the specified error ring/reason and never
enter a song update. Invalid drafts revert on blur. Valid titles share ui-23's
one timer/write queue with lyrics and save on idle, blur, or Enter. Own replies
retain newer title input. A raced duplicate refusal marks the title while valid
lyrics still save under its canonical title. Lyric undo preserves accepted title
metadata.

Forward the library selection through
`store.open(selection.document, {focusTitle: selection.focusTitle})`. The header
consumes the request once, focuses/selects the title, supports a fresh request
for the same song, and does not refocus on panel remount.

This request stops at the title and two tags to keep the P2b split small.
Default background, 구성, preview, and the slide list follow in later requests.
