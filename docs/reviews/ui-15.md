## What changed and why

The design-system glossary still required 장 for slides, while the chosen
product term is 슬라이드. The glossary and component READMEs now use 슬라이드,
with an explicit exception for Bible chapter references such as 창세기 1장 and
요한복음 3장 16절. Slide counts use "슬라이드 9개" and positions retain "2 / 9".

The arrangement insert button now says "슬라이드 넣기" in its accessible name
and tooltip. Gallery panels, recovery choices, import controls, count metadata,
and their design-system cards use the same terminology. A Bible item's title
keeps "요한복음 3장 16절" while its separate count becomes "슬라이드 2개". The
Bible reference-placement setting becomes "슬라이드마다", as spec 10 defines it
per output slide, not per Bible chapter.

Changes are made to the owned labels and examples at their sources. Components
continue to display caller-provided titles. 저장, 장치, 가장자리, durations,
positions, command identifiers, and stored data retain their meanings.
