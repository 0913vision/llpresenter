## What changed and why

Only ThumbnailSizeControl restored Show keyboard focus after pointer use. A
generic volume Slider kept focus, letting its next keyboard command operate the
slider. Slider now accepts `workspace`, defaulting to `prep`. With `show`, a
finished pointer interaction returns focus to its previous owner when focus is
still on the slider. Tab can focus it normally. A newer focus destination wins,
and a missing previous owner leaves the slider blurred.

`useSliderPointer` listens for pointer completion on the window during the
gesture, including a release outside the input. Capture loss alone does not end
the drag: only `pointerup`, `pointercancel`, or window blur commits. Losing
window focus commits without bringing focus back. The current callback is used
after rerenders.

Each gesture has one AbortController for its three temporary listeners. They are
removed at completion, window blur, a workspace or disabled-state change, and
unmount. No timer, cache, dependency, token, IPC, or shell wiring is added.
ThumbnailSizeControl forwards its workspace and reuses this implementation.

The gallery has a live note field and Show volume slider. The Slider README and
preview card describe the behavior. The click-focus selector stays unchanged:
native range handling remains enabled.

The ui-16 nit is included: NumberField TSDoc and the TextField README say
`largeStep` should be a whole multiple of `step`. This is documentation only.
The reviewer's correction is retained: Enter rounds a typed draft; after an
arrow press clears that draft, Enter does not change the value.

## Decisions I made (for the lead to confirm)

Retain native range dragging and restore focus when the gesture ends. Preserve a
focus change made during that gesture. Window blur commits without restoring
focus; a workspace or disabled-state change aborts the pending interaction. Keep
the default workspace as `prep`, and pass `show` at Show callers.
